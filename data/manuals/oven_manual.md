# ThermaBake OV-500 Convection Oven — User Manual

## Overview
The ThermaBake OV-500 is a freestanding electric oven with true convection, a
5-liter steam-assist reservoir, and 8 cooking modes: Bake, Broil, Convection Bake,
Convection Roast, Proof, Dehydrate, Self-Clean, and Air Fry.

## Safety Warnings
- The exterior surfaces and oven door become hot during use; use oven mitts.
- Do not line the oven bottom with foil; it blocks airflow and can trap heat, causing damage.
- Keep flammable materials (towels, curtains, packaging) away from the oven while in use.
- During Self-Clean mode, the door locks automatically and interior temperatures exceed
  480°C (900°F) — do not attempt to force the door open.

## Control Panel
- **Mode Dial**: Rotate to select Bake, Broil, Convection, Proof, Dehydrate, Air Fry,
  or Self-Clean.
- **Temperature**: Press "Temp" and use +/- or the numeric pad to set target temperature
  (65°C–290°C / 150°F–550°F).
- **Convection Fan**: Automatically engages in Convection modes; reduces suggested
  temperature by about 20°C (25°F) versus conventional Bake for the same result.
- **Preheat Indicator**: A tone sounds and the display stops flashing once the target
  temperature is reached.

## Cooking Guidelines
- For roasting meats, use Convection Roast for more even browning and shorter cook times.
- For baking multiple trays, use Convection Bake and rotate trays halfway through.
- Air Fry mode circulates high-speed hot air; use the supplied perforated basket for
  best crisping results, and shake or flip food halfway through cooking.
- Always preheat before baking delicate items like cakes or soufflés.

## Cleaning & Maintenance
- Run Self-Clean monthly for heavy use, or every 2–3 months for light use.
- Remove oven racks before starting Self-Clean; they can discolor at extreme temperatures.
- Wipe up spills after the oven cools to prevent baked-on residue.
- Clean the door glass with a non-abrasive glass cleaner only.

## Troubleshooting
| Symptom | Likely Cause | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Oven won't heat | Igniter or heating element failure | Contact service; do not attempt DIY repair on heating elements |
| Uneven baking | Fan blocked or oven not preheated fully | Clear vents, wait for full preheat tone |
| Door won't unlock after Self-Clean | Interior still above safe threshold | Wait for full cool-down cycle (up to 1 hour) |
| Error code E1 | Temperature sensor fault | Power cycle the unit; contact service if persistent |

## Specifications
- Capacity: 90L | Power: 240V, 30A | Max Temp: 290°C (550°F) | Weight: 45 kg
