# CrispWave TS-4 4-Slice Toaster — User Manual

## Overview
The CrispWave TS-4 is a 4-slice, 2-long-slot toaster with 7 browning levels, a
Bagel setting, a Defrost setting, and a Reheat setting.

## Safety Warnings
- Never insert metal utensils into the toaster slots while plugged in; this risks
  electric shock.
- Do not toast items wrapped in foil or with butter/glaze already applied.
- Do not leave the toaster unattended while operating; unplug when not in use.
- Ensure nothing is stored on top of the toaster, as vents release hot air during toasting.

## Control Panel
- **Browning Dial (1–7)**: Higher numbers produce darker toast.
- **Bagel Button**: Toasts one side at high heat while lightly warming the other —
  ideal for split bagels.
- **Defrost Button**: Adds extra time to toast frozen bread evenly.
- **Reheat Button**: Warms already-toasted bread without further browning.
- **Cancel Button**: Immediately stops toasting and pops up the carriage.

## Usage Guidelines
- For even toasting, use bread slices of consistent thickness.
- Clean the crumb tray regularly to prevent smoke or fire risk.
- If bread gets stuck, unplug the unit first, then gently remove with a wooden utensil.

## Cleaning & Maintenance
- Unplug and let cool before cleaning.
- Slide out the crumb tray from the bottom and empty it after every few uses.
- Wipe the exterior with a soft, dry cloth; do not submerge the unit in water.

## Troubleshooting
| Symptom | Likely Cause | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Toast too light/dark | Browning dial set incorrectly | Adjust dial and retoast |
| Carriage won't stay down | Bread jam or mechanical fault | Clear jam; contact service if it persists |
| Uneven toasting | Slots blocked by crumbs | Clean crumb tray and slot interior |
| No power | Outlet issue or tripped breaker | Test outlet with another appliance |

## Specifications
- Wattage: 1500W | Slots: 4 (2 long) | Voltage: 120V | Weight: 2.1 kg
