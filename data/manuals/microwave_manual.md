# CompactChef MW-720 Microwave Oven — User Manual

## Overview
The CompactChef MW-720 is a 900-watt countertop microwave oven with 10 power levels,
a 30-liter stainless steel cavity, and 6 auto-cook presets (Popcorn, Beverage, Pizza,
Potato, Frozen Dinner, Reheat).

## Safety Warnings
- Never operate the microwave empty; this can damage the magnetron.
- Do not place metal containers, aluminum foil, or twist ties inside the cavity.
- Do not attempt to heat sealed containers (eggs in shell, sealed jars) as they may
  explode from pressure buildup.
- If the door seal or hinges are damaged, discontinue use and contact service support.
- Children under 8 should only use the microwave under adult supervision.

## Control Panel
- **Power Level (1–10)**: Press "Power" then a number key. Level 10 = full power (900W).
- **Time Entry**: Enter cooking time using the numeric keypad, then press "Start".
- **Auto Cook**: Select a preset icon and enter the approximate weight; the unit
  calculates time and power automatically.
- **Defrost**: Enter weight in grams; the unit alternates power cycles to defrost evenly.
- **Clock/Timer**: Press "Clock" to set the time of day, or "Timer" for a kitchen timer
  that does not activate the microwave.

## Cooking Guidelines
- For reheating leftovers, use Power Level 7 and stir at the halfway point for even heating.
- For popcorn, use the dedicated Popcorn button; do not exceed the recommended bag size.
- Standing time after cooking allows heat to distribute evenly — do not skip this step
  for dense foods like casseroles.

## Cleaning & Maintenance
- Unplug the unit before cleaning.
- Wipe the interior with a damp cloth and mild detergent; avoid abrasive scrubbers.
- The turntable and roller ring are dishwasher safe.
- Clean the door seal regularly — food debris here can prevent proper sealing.

## Troubleshooting
| Symptom | Likely Cause | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Turntable not rotating | Roller ring misaligned | Reseat roller ring and turntable |
| No power | Circuit tripped or door not fully closed | Check breaker; ensure door latch clicks |
| Sparking inside | Metal object or damaged interior paint | Remove metal, stop use, contact service if paint is damaged |
| Uneven heating | Power level too high or no stirring | Lower power level, stir food halfway |

## Specifications
- Wattage: 900W | Capacity: 30L | Voltage: 120V, 60Hz | Weight: 12.5 kg
