# BrewPoint CM-12 Programmable Coffee Maker — User Manual

## Overview
The BrewPoint CM-12 is a 12-cup programmable drip coffee maker with a 24-hour
brew timer, a reusable gold-tone filter, and a keep-warm plate with auto shut-off.

## Safety Warnings
- Do not operate without water in the reservoir; this can damage the heating element.
- The carafe and warming plate become hot during and after brewing — use the handle.
- Do not use the glass carafe on a stovetop or in a microwave.
- Descale regularly; mineral buildup can cause overheating and reduce lifespan.

## Control Panel
- **Program Button**: Set the hour and minute for the next automatic brew.
- **Brew Strength**: Toggle between Regular and Bold for a stronger extraction.
- **Auto Shut-Off**: The warming plate turns off automatically after 2 hours.
- **Pause & Serve**: Allows removing the carafe mid-brew for up to 20 seconds without
  dripping; do not leave it out longer or the filter basket may overflow.

## Usage Guidelines
- Use 1 tablespoon of ground coffee per cup for standard strength; adjust to taste.
- Use filtered water where possible to reduce mineral buildup and improve flavor.
- Preheat is not required — the unit begins heating as soon as brewing starts.

## Cleaning & Maintenance
- Rinse the reusable filter after every use; wash weekly with mild soap.
- Descale every 2–3 months using a 1:1 water-and-vinegar solution or a commercial
  descaling solution, followed by two rinse cycles with plain water.
- Wipe the warming plate with a damp cloth once fully cooled.

## Troubleshooting
| Symptom | Likely Cause | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Coffee too weak | Insufficient grounds or wrong strength setting | Increase grounds, select Bold |
| Brewing very slow | Mineral scale buildup | Run a descaling cycle |
| Overflow at filter basket | Carafe removed too long during Pause & Serve | Replace carafe promptly, limit pauses to under 20 seconds |
| Coffee maker won't turn on | Outlet issue or programmed timer not yet reached | Check outlet; verify clock/timer settings |

## Specifications
- Capacity: 12 cups (1.8L) | Wattage: 1100W | Voltage: 120V | Weight: 2.3 kg
