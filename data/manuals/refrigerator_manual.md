# FreshHold RF-360 French Door Refrigerator — User Manual

## Overview
The FreshHold RF-360 is a 360-liter French door refrigerator with a bottom freezer,
adjustable humidity crisper drawers, an ice maker, and a digital temperature display.

## Safety Warnings
- Allow the unit to stand upright for at least 4 hours before first power-on if it
  was transported on its side, to let refrigerant settle.
- Do not store flammable materials (aerosols, solvents) inside the unit.
- Keep the door seals clean and undamaged; a poor seal wastes energy and reduces
  cooling performance.
- Disconnect power before replacing the internal light bulb.

## Control Panel
- **Fridge Temp**: Adjustable from 1°C to 7°C (recommended: 4°C / 37°F).
- **Freezer Temp**: Adjustable from -22°C to -15°C (recommended: -18°C / 0°F).
- **Ice Maker Toggle**: Turns the automatic ice maker on/off; allow 24 hours after
  first activation for ice production to begin.
- **Door Alarm**: Sounds if a door is left ajar for more than 60 seconds.
- **Vacation Mode**: Reduces energy use while keeping the freezer fully functional.

## Usage Guidelines
- Do not overpack shelves; airflow around vents keeps temperature even.
- Store raw meat on lower shelves to prevent drip contamination.
- Crisper drawer humidity: high humidity for leafy greens, low humidity for fruit
  that produces ethylene gas (apples, pears).
- Allow hot food to cool before placing it inside to reduce compressor strain.

## Cleaning & Maintenance
- Vacuum the condenser coils (usually at the back or bottom) every 6 months.
- Clean interior shelves with mild soap and warm water; avoid ammonia-based cleaners.
- Replace the water filter every 6 months for units with an ice/water dispenser.
- Defrost the freezer manually only if frost buildup exceeds 5mm (most models are frost-free).

## Troubleshooting
| Symptom | Likely Cause | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Fridge not cooling | Vents blocked or condenser coils dirty | Clear vents, clean coils |
| Ice maker not producing | Water line not connected or filter clogged | Check water supply, replace filter |
| Water pooling inside | Drain tube clogged | Clear drain tube with warm water |
| Excessive noise | Unit not level or compressor cycling normally | Level the unit using adjustable feet |

## Specifications
- Capacity: 360L (250L fridge / 110L freezer) | Voltage: 120V | Weight: 95 kg
